# Original shell artwork

Nebula and Botanical were generated with the built-in image-generation tool
on October 16, 2026, then copied into this project as nebula.png and botanical.png.
They are original galaxy-inspired/botanical prints, not copies of Nintendo
shell artwork. No exclusivity is claimed. Other patterns are authored in code.

Nebula prompt: final flat full-bleed landscape artwork for a small premium
handheld-console lid, not a product photo; midnight indigo/violet nebula,
lavender clouds, restrained cyan highlights, sparse delicate stars; no planets,
lettering, logos, hardware, border, perspective, shadows or material reflections.

Botanical prompt: final flat full-bleed landscape artwork for a handheld lid,
not a console photo; elegant ivory cosmos flowers, dusty-rose buds, sage and
eucalyptus leaves on desaturated deep teal, fine stems and painted petals,
generous dark spaces; no hardware, perspective, photographed shadows, text,
logos or watermark. Landscape 16:9, matte printed appearance.

Consumers: shell_catalog.gd, shell_finish.gdshader and shell_swatch.gdshader.
The same selected palette controls the inner deck and exterior printed finish.
